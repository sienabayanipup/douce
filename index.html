About Us

Douce en Fleur is a small flower shop in the Philippines, inspired by the quiet elegance of French floristry and grounded in the warmth of home.

We work with local growers and only stock what is fresh and in season, so every bouquet reflects what is blooming nearby right now.

Every bouquet is arranged by hand and wrapped with care, whether it is for a birthday, a celebration or simply a quiet thank you.

“Buy fewer flowers, buy them fresher, and let them look like they grew together.”
